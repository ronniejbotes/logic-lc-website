About Logic LC

Your Nationwide Security, Networking and Technology Partner

Since 2008, Logic LC has helped businesses and organizations design, install and support reliable technology systems across the United States. Privately owned and headquartered in the Philadelphia area, we are a nationwide technology integrator specializing in commercial security systems, video surveillance, access control, structured cabling, networking, Wi-Fi and low-voltage infrastructure.

Our mission is simple: deliver innovative technology solutions that protect people, connect operations and help businesses grow.

Complete Technology Solutions—from Design to Deployment

Logic LC provides end-to-end technology services for individual facilities and large, multi-location organizations. Our experienced team works with clients from the initial consultation and system design through installation, programming, training and ongoing support.

Our capabilities include:

  • Commercial security camera systems
  • Cloud-based video surveillance
  • Door access control and intercom systems
  • Intrusion alarms and environmental monitoring
  • Structured cabling and fiber-optic installation
  • Network infrastructure and equipment deployment
  • Business Wi-Fi and wireless network solutions
  • Nationwide technology rollouts
  • Site surveys, system design and project management
  • Ongoing technical support and system maintenance

Whether we are upgrading an existing surveillance system, securing a new facility, installing network cabling or coordinating a nationwide deployment, our team approaches every project with precision, accountability and attention to detail.

Built for Businesses with One Location—or Hundreds

Technology projects become more complex when they involve multiple buildings, locations, vendors and timelines. Logic LC provides a single, dependable partner capable of managing projects locally and throughout the lower 48 states.

We serve businesses and organizations across a wide range of industries, including commercial real estate, manufacturing, retail, hospitality, healthcare, education, construction and multi-location enterprises.

Our team maintains consistent installation standards, documentation and communication across every project. This gives our clients greater visibility, fewer surprises and technology systems that perform reliably from one location to the next.

More Than an Installation Company

For more than 18 years, Logic LC has built its reputation by solving difficult problems and developing lasting client relationships. We are engineers, system designers, project managers, technicians and support professionals united by a commitment to doing the job correctly.

We take the time to understand each client’s facilities, operations, security concerns and long-term goals. Instead of offering a one-size-fits-all package, we design practical solutions around the organization’s actual needs.

Our clients choose Logic LC because we provide:

  • One trusted partner for security, networking and low-voltage systems
  • Nationwide installation and project-management capabilities
  • Experienced technical professionals
  • Customized system design and deployment
  • Clear communication and detailed documentation
  • Reliable post-installation training and support
  • Long-term partnerships built on trust and performance
