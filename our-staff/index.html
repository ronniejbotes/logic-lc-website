Our Vision & Mission

Technology That Protects, Connects and Supports Your Business.

Logic LC’s mission is simple: deliver innovative technology solutions that protect people, connect operations and help businesses grow. Since 2008, our team has helped businesses and organizations design, install and support reliable technology systems across the United States.

Delivering Complete Technology Solutions

From initial consultation and system design through installation, programming, training and ongoing support, Logic LC provides end-to-end technology services for businesses of all sizes.

Protecting People & Property

We provide commercial security cameras, cloud-based video surveillance, access control, intrusion alarms and environmental monitoring designed around each organization's specific needs.

Keeping Businesses Connected

Our networking, structured cabling, fiber-optic and business Wi-Fi solutions help organizations maintain reliable, secure and high-performing technology infrastructure.

Supporting Businesses Long-Term

Our relationship doesn't end after installation. We provide ongoing technical support, system maintenance and project management to help technology continue performing reliably over time.
